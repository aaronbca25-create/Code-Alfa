The Seed of Greatness

Every giant tree begins as a tiny seed.

Long ago, a young girl named Maya lived in a town surrounded by mountains. She dreamed of becoming a famous artist, but everyone around her believed art could never become a real career.

Whenever she showed her drawings, people would say, "Focus on something practical." Their words hurt, but Maya continued sketching every day.

One day, an old gardener noticed her sitting beneath a tree, discouraged after receiving criticism. He handed her a tiny seed and said, "Plant this."

Maya planted the seed and watered it every day. Weeks passed, and nothing appeared. Yet she continued caring for it.

"Growth happens long before it becomes visible."

Months later, a small sprout emerged. Over the years, it grew into a strong tree. Watching it grow taught Maya an important lesson: meaningful things take time.

Inspired, she treated her artistic dream the same way she treated the seed. She practiced daily, learned from mistakes, and improved little by little.

Years later, Maya's artwork was displayed in galleries across the country. People admired her talent, unaware of the years of unseen effort behind her success.

Success is like a seed. With patience, hard work, and faith in yourself, it eventually grows into something extraordinary.
Never underestimate small beginnings.
